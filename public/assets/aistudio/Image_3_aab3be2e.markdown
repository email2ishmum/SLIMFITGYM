## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an empowering, premium, and safe sanctuary aesthetic tailored specifically for a female-only fitness center in Mirpur-2, Dhaka. The visual narrative combines modern athletic precision with refined editorial poise. It avoids cliché feminine tropes in favor of confident strength, bodily autonomy, and private luxury.

The style fuses High-Contrast Editorial structure with Tactile Warmth:
- Crisp geometric typography and obsidian headlines anchor the space with elite, professional gravitas.
- Energizing rose-magenta accents cut through muted, warm canvas tones to direct momentum and action.
- Spacious editorial pacing and whisper-soft blush sections establish an environment that feels private, dignified, and welcoming.

## Colors

The palette balances deep athletic contrast with comforting hospitality tones:

- **Canvas & Backing (`#FAF8F6`):** A warm off-white foundation that eliminates harsh digital glare while feeling clean and serene.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white reserved for interactive cards, schedule widgets, and modular containers.
- **Alternating Section Blush (`#FDF0F4`):** A barely-there warm rose wash used on alternating tiers (such as pricing, amenities, or trainer profiles) to break monotony without adding visual weight.
- **Headings & Primary Structure (`#161416`):** Deep obsidian charcoal derived from the physical gym's polished black-and-silver branding. Used for dominant headings, stark dividers, and high-impact micro-copy.
- **Brand Accent & Primary Action (`#D92662`):** Vibrant rose-magenta designed for high-conversion CTAs, active status indicators, and energetic highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B81D50`):** Deeper velvet rose for pressed states, badges, and gradient terminal stops.
- **Body & Secondary Copy (`#4A4448`):** Deep charcoal slate with balanced warmth, ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against both white and blush surfaces.
- **Hairline Borders (`#E7E3E5`):** Subtle warm structural lines for card frames and divider rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates athletic authority and editorial care:

- **Headlines:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tight negative tracking and bold weights, echoing modern wellness editorial mastheads.
- **Body & Running Text:** Set in `Inter` for functional clarity, tabular figure alignment in membership rates, and effortless reading on mobile viewports.
- **Bilingual & Localization Support:** For authentic Bengali member reviews and localized Mirpur-2 schedules, pair body text with `Hind Siliguri`. Maintain equivalent optical scale by pairing `Hind Siliguri` at 1.05x the point size of `Inter` to match baseline heights accurately.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or high-contrast small labels leverage `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter-spacing (+0.05em) for crisp categorization badges (e.g., "FEMALE TRAINERS ONLY", "MIRPUR-2 BRANCH").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8pt modular scale embedded within a 12-column responsive grid on desktop and a 4-column structure on mobile devices:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at `1280px` for optimal reading scanning.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` margins, `1rem` gutters.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** Alternating content bands utilize generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` multiplied by 2 or 3) to convey privacy, premium exclusivity, and unhurried luxury.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through soft physical surface separation rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:** Cards sit on `#FFFFFF` against the `#FAF8F6` canvas, demarcated by a 1px border in `#E7E3E5`.
- **Ambient Lift:** Elevated components (such as sticky booking bars or active pricing cards) feature an ultra-diffused rose-tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(22, 20, 22, 0.05), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(217, 38, 98, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Hover:** Cards and buttons subtly elevate by -2px with an intensified ambient magenta glow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(217, 38, 98, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric balance:
- **Cards and Modals:** Feature a controlled `12px` to `16px` radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), creating soft yet architecturally precise containers.
- **Interactive Badges & CTAs:** Primary buttons, category tags, and pill chips use fully rounded radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to soften high-contrast action triggers and feel ergonomic for mobile thumb taps.
- **Visual Media:** Trainer portraits, facility photography, and privacy feature spotlights take a matching `16px` corner radius with an inset 1px stroke (`rgba(22, 20, 22, 0.06)`) to ensure crisp perimeter definition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Join Now / Book Trial):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), `#D92662` background, pure `#FFFFFF` label in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. On hover, transitions to `#B81D50` with an ambient rose shadow.
- **Secondary (Explore Schedule):** Pill silhouette with a solid obsidian border (`#161416`, 1.5px), transparent fill, and `#161416` text. Fills with `#161416` and white text on hover.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Text link with a high-contrast underline that animates to `#D92662` on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped tags with `#FDF0F4` background, 1px border in `#D92662`/20%, and `#D92662` text in `label-sm`. Used for flags like "100% LADIES ONLY", "CERTIFIED NUTRITIONIST", and "MIRPUR-2".
- **Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` background with `#E7E3E5` borders. Active state fills with `#161416` and turns text to `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards (Classes, Trainers, Memberships)
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px border (`#E7E3E5`).
- Internal padding set to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- Highlighted or featured cards (e.g., "VIP Membership") receive a 2px `#D92662` top highlight or full border and a subtle floating shadow.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Solid `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E7E3E5` borders and a `12px` corner radius.
- Placeholder text in `#4A4448` at 60% opacity.
- Focus state triggers an crisp 1.5px `#D92662` stroke and a soft 3px magenta halo (`rgba(217, 38, 98, 0.15)`).

### Privacy & Assurance Notices
- Dedicated callout banners with `#FDF0F4` background, rounded `16px` borders, subtle rose edge treatment, and a dark obsidian lock or privacy badge icon to assure 100% female-only staff and privacy.

### Testimonial Cards
- Features dual-language typographic support: `Inter` for English metrics and `Hind Siliguri` for authentic Bengali member reflections, enclosed in clean white cards with warm gray divider accents.
